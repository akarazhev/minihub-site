What DAOs actually fund

Who notices when a price feed goes wrong

1,163 commits matching six feed-failure terms across 48 crypto organisations. A hand check of 100 diffs found about one in six is an actual repair — the rest is documentation, CI and “stale” in its other meanings. The correction and every classification are in the repository.

A job that forgets which method it was asked to run

Odoo's queue_job recorded the __name__ of the function it found, not the name it was asked for, so a module that installs a replacement under another name, as auditlog does for write and unlink, breaks the job when it runs. The name was lost in four places; the fix and three tests are in #998.
